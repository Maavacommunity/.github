## 👋 About MAAVA

**MAAVA** is a community for developers and students who want to learn by doing. We contribute to existing open-source projects, ship new ones together, and push into some of the most exciting frontiers in tech today — **AI, Agents & MCPs**, **AI Skills**, and **Blockchain & Web3**.

We believe the best way to grow as an engineer is to build in public, alongside people who are a little ahead, a little behind, and right beside you — and that a thriving open-source culture is something we all have to actively keep alive.

> 🎯 **Our mission:** empower open-source culture — help people learn, contribute, and build real things — and make sure the value we create gives back.

## 💛 Why We Give Back

MAAVA isn't just about code — it's about impact beyond our own careers.

When a project or repo built within our community starts generating income, **a portion of that profit is set aside and distributed** to support:

- 🍲 **Fighting world hunger**
- 📚 **Education and support for orphaned children**

We think technology should do more than pay salaries — it should lift up people who never got a fair shot. Building skills, building software, and building a better world aren't separate goals for us; they're the same goal.

## 🤝 How to Contribute

1. ⭐ **Star and watch** this org to stay in the loop on new projects.
2. 💬 **Join our Discord** and say hi — tell us what you want to learn or build.
3. 🔎 Look for issues labeled **`good first issue`** or **`help wanted`** across our repos.
4. 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Pick a track — **Open Source**, **AI/Agents/MCP**, or **Web3** — and join that working group.
5. 🚀 Have an idea for a new project? Open a proposal issue in the `.github` repo and pitch it to the community.

New to open source? Perfect — that's exactly who MAAVA is for. Come learn with us.

<br/>

## 🌱 Our Values

- **Learn in public** — questions are welcome, mistakes are how we grow.
- **Ship real things** — we optimize for working code and real contributions over perfect plans.
- **Own the culture** — open source only survives if people give back to it, not just take from it.
- **Give back beyond code** — success here is shared with people who need it most.
